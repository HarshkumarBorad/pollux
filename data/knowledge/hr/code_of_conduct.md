# Aurora Labs — Code of Conduct

All Aurora Labs employees, contractors, and visitors follow this Code at company premises and at any company-sponsored event.

## Respect
We treat each other with respect regardless of role, background, or seniority. Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation in any form is not tolerated.

## Reporting harassment
You can report through any of:

1. **Your manager** or skip-level manager.
2. **HR partner** — contact details in the HR portal.
3. **Anonymous ethics hotline** — `ethics.aurora.example` or +49 30 1234 5678.
4. **Works council (Betriebsrat)** — for collective concerns.

All reports are investigated. Retaliation against people who report in good faith is itself a violation of this Code.

## Confidentiality
Employees handling personal data, customer information, or trade secrets must follow the Data Protection Policy and the GDPR. Unauthorized disclosure may result in termination and legal action.

Customer data must only be accessed for legitimate business reasons. All production database access is logged.

## Outside employment
Allowed provided it does not conflict with Aurora interests or interfere with your work performance. Must be disclosed to HR in writing.

## Conflicts of interest
Disclose any relationship (family, financial, romantic) with vendors, customers, or candidates that could create a conflict. Disclosures via the Conflicts portal.

## Substance policy
Alcohol is permitted at sanctioned company events in moderation. Illegal drugs are not permitted on company premises at any time. Employees who appear impaired at work will be sent home and may be subject to disciplinary action.
