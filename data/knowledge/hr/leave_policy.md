# Aurora Labs — Leave Policy

Effective from 1 January 2025. Applies to all full-time employees in Germany.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive **30 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to **5 unused days** can carry over and must be taken by 31 March.

Requested via the HR portal, approved by your direct manager.

## Sick leave
Up to **6 weeks** of paid sick leave at full pay, per illness. Beyond 6 weeks: statutory sickness benefits (Krankengeld) from the public health insurance fund.

A medical certificate ("Krankschreibung") is required **from day 3** onwards. For sick leave longer than 3 calendar days, submit to HR via the portal within 7 days of issuance.

## Parental leave
German Elternzeit framework: up to **3 years** of unpaid leave per parent, per child. Parental allowance (Elterngeld) is paid by the government for the first 14 months, independent of Aurora's pay.

Notify HR at least 7 weeks before intended start.

## Sabbatical
After **5 years** of continuous employment, employees may apply for a sabbatical of up to **3 months**. Approval at manager discretion, depending on team workload. Paid (deducted from accrued time) or unpaid.

## Bereavement leave
- Spouse, child, or parent: 5 working days paid.
- Sibling or grandparent: 2 working days paid.
- In-law: 1 working day paid.

## How to request leave
1. Open the HR portal at `hr.aurora.example`.
2. **My Leave → Request leave**.
3. Pick the leave type, dates, and short reason for sick / unpaid leave.
4. Manager approves or declines within 3 business days.

## Public holidays
Aurora Labs observes all German federal public holidays. Office closure between **24 December and 1 January** is paid and does not count against your annual leave.
