# Aurora Labs — Onboarding Handbook

Welcome to Aurora Labs. This handbook covers your first month.

## Day 1
- Pick up your laptop from the IT desk on Floor 3 between 09:00 and 10:00.
- HR will email your accounts list within 24 hours. Activate via the SSO portal at `sso.aurora.example`.
- Attend the 09:30 orientation in the Lakeshore Room (Floor 5).

## First-week checklist
1. Complete mandatory compliance training: GDPR, information security, anti-harassment. All courses are in the Learning Portal and take ~3 hours combined.
2. 1:1 with your manager — booked within your first 48 hours.
3. 1:1 with your HR partner — booked by HR for end of week 1.
4. Set up your development environment using the Engineering Setup Guide.
5. Join the relevant Slack channels for your team and tooling.

## Accounts you will receive
- Aurora SSO (email, calendar, drive).
- GitHub Enterprise organization.
- Slack workspace.
- Aurora internal cloud console (`console.aurora.example`).
- VPN credentials (delivered separately by IT).

## Buddy program
Every new hire is paired with a "buddy" from a different team. Your buddy answers informal questions during your first month and is not in your reporting line.

## Probation
The standard probation period is **six months**. Monthly check-ins with your manager. At the end, your manager conducts a formal performance review.

## Working hours
Trust-based model. Core hours are 10:00–16:00. Remote work up to 3 days per week is supported for all eligible roles.

## Reporting and escalations
For onboarding issues: `onboarding@aurora.example` or your HR partner. Urgent technical access issues: helpdesk portal at `helpdesk.aurora.example`.
